Each rat wears its own IMU and is tracked on its own: rat 1 is object 1 in the masks (red: skull, trajectory, mask tint), rat 2 is object 2 (blue). Untick a rat to track only the other one.

Tracking uses every camera's masks: the rat's 3D position is triangulated and its body axis fused across views. Orientation is the IMU's own pitch and roll (gravity-referenced); its heading drifts, so it is re-aligned at anchors every N frames to the fused body axis (nose/tail from the walking direction). Small N follows drift closely but pulls the head's heading towards the body's; large N keeps head-vs-body turns but lets drift build up between anchors. The status line shows a held-out score for the N you chose.

An IMU glued on at an angle makes the head look permanently rolled. Auto removes the constant tilt that puts the head level on average while walking (on this recording about 32 deg of roll); the values it found are shown after the run and can be typed in under manual.

The median view removes each camera's parallax smear of the rat (it needs 3 or more videos); a single camera view shows the rat stretched away from that camera, since the warp is exact only on the floor. The floor is not at the calibration's z=0: blank estimates its height as the plane where the cameras' rat-free backgrounds line up best.
