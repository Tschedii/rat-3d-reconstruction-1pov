From the camera rig to a rat's first-person view

55 s · recording frames 220 to 1000 · rendered in Blender from the outputs of GUIs 1 to 5.2

  • Reconstruction: seed point from the mask centroids, voxel carving camera by camera, the visual hull's surface, then the fitted rat template (what it adds in colour) and the fur textured from the cameras.
  • IMU heads: head orientation from each rat's IMU, head position tracked from the six cameras' masks.
  • First-person view: rat 1's eye camera (eyes marked in GUI 3, GUI 4's 120° × 133° view), gimbal-stabilised, frames 750 to 1000, with rat 2 labelled whenever it is in view.
